Spiritual healer in the East Bay

Year2026
ClientCecilia H. Cardoza
RoleDirection, photography, UX/UI, code
Built withHTML, CSS, JS, Photoshop
01

The brief

Cece is a curandera offering tarot, limpias and family care planning. She needed a site that feels true to her practice, not a pastel wellness template, and still makes booking easy.

02

What I did

Photographed Cece, built the palette and identity from those photos, then designed and coded the site around them.

03

The result

A dark, reverent site with small rituals built into the experience, from a card of the day to booking steps lit drop by drop.

The full site

Every section

The whole page, desktop and mobile. Click any strip to enlarge.

Interactions

Small rituals built into the page. Recorded on a phone.

01 / 07

Booking, drop by drop

A gold drop falls through the five booking steps as you scroll, lighting each step it passes, so visitors always see where they are.

02 / 07

The veil lifts

Her opening quote starts blurred, as if behind the veil, and clears word by word. The key words settle into gold.

03 / 07

A card for today

Tap the deck to draw a card. Sixteen short messages, with a link to book a full reading right under it.

04 / 07

Sage smoke

Smoke drifts up from the sage bundle and past the arched frame, a quiet moment beside her words.

05 / 07

Candle intentions

Pick one of four intentions and the candle card takes on its color, with a line on what it is for.

06 / 07

Where to start

Three quick questions point to the right service, then pre-fill the contact form with it.

07 / 07

Hella Healed

The motto burns in, left to right, as the page closes.

Mobile

Built for the phone

Every section was laid out for the phone first: text before image, nothing to pinch or zoom.
