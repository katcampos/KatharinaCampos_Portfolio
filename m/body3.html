MaracatuRhythm

A seven-panel booklet about the Caboclo de Lança, the lance-carrying guardian of Maracatu Rural from Pernambuco, Brazil.

Year2026
Format5×5 in accordion booklet + AR
RolePhotography, papercut, layout & stop motion

01

The object

A miniature Caboclo de Lança, a gift from my mom. He comes from the sugarcane fields of Pernambuco, where the cutter’s tool became a lance and the lance became a dance of protection.

02

The story

For over a century, through exploited labor, poverty and neglect, he has kept walking into carnaval. The book is about that persistence: resistance carried as celebration.

03

The book

Photo and text panels alternate like call and response. Handmade glitter papercuts repeat like a beat, and the last panel comes alive in AR.

The sequence

Six images, six stages

The viewer meets him, gets pulled into his color and motion until he dissolves into carnaval, then comes back to him as a living archive. Each photo sits beside its text. DK Downward Fall, a hand-cut display face, bounces like the dance; Charming Sixties adds the warmth of festival signage. The primary colors come from his chita fabric.

Motion extension · Artivive

Augmented reality

The final text, “Born from ordinary people, kept alive as collective resistance,” triggers an 11-second stop motion. The overlays in the book are handmade papercuts, so the animation is made the same way: flag, ribbons, mask and carnation slide in one by one. The book ends in movement instead of a full stop, the way the tradition survives: by continuing.

Process

Behind the work

From the object on my desk to a printed, folding booklet with a moving last page.

01

Object & research

I studied what he carries: the lança swung as protection, the cravo in his mouth that closes the body against harm, the loud chita fabric. Then I took notes from interviews and documentaries about Maracatu Rural.

02

Two photoshoots

Six setups across two shoots. The first on glitter, a silver patterned backdrop, and ferns with dried flowers. The second after dark: spin blur, then red and teal gels through haze.

03

Handmade elements

Glitter-paper cutouts of the Pernambuco and Brasil flags, a sun, spirals, stars and a paper rose, photographed and layered onto the pages.

04

Layout & prototypes

Frame options on the wall for critique, then four printed prototypes, including a mini one, to test size, folding and how the panels pair.

05

Stop motion

I cut the paper caboclo into pieces and shot him frame by frame under a ring light. The frames became the AR animation.
